# Functional Design Document (FDD)

The [Rijksmuseum](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/) is a Dutch national museum dedicated to arts and history in Amsterdam.
It has a large collection of art pieces from the Dutch Golden Age, including works by Vermeer and Rembrandt.

Visitors can explore the collection by visiting the [museum's website](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/nl/zoeken)
However, the Rijksmuseum wants a more innovative way to explore the collection. For example as a game.

Your multidisciplinary team consisting of designers and developers, is asked to come up with a solution.

The designers will explore the possibilities and come up with a design that will be used as a basis for the development.
Of course, the developers will have to make sure that the design is feasible and can be implemented in  an early stage.


## Functional requirements
* Come up with a new innovative way to explore the collection (MV).
* Develop a new application for the Rijksmuseum (SD):
  * Use images from the Rijksmuseum collection.
  * Make sure you comply with image copyright requirements (https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/nl/onderzoek/onderzoek-doen/data/beleid)
  * A game that mirrors the design created by the designers.

## Non-functional requirements
* The application should be tested.
* The application should be optimized for performance.

When you click on a piece of the painting that doesn't fit there you get it in your inventory and when your on the painting that the
piece is suposed to be on and click on the piece it goes in the painting.

Menu's: The menu's are made to make the game look nicer and feel more profecional and inform the players about the makers and paintings. 
Made by dean.

Funcionality of the game: 
* Character can walk left and right.
* You can click on paintings to zoom in on them and the inventory automatically opens.
* If you click on the wall behind the painting or on 'esc' on your keyboard you exit the painting zoom.
* If you click on a piece of the painting that doesn't fit there you get it in your inventory.
* When you click on the piece in your inventory and are on the coresponding painting it goes back into that painting in the right place. 
* If all pieces are collected you win the game and are moved to another scene. 
Coded by Alex.
